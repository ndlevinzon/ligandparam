# NNNN — Short title (verb phrase preferred)

> **Status:** Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Reversed
> **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
> **Author:** <github-handle>
> **Affects:** <which modules / which repos>

## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play? Why is a decision needed
now? Keep this section short — enough for a future reader to understand the
pressure that produced the decision, not a full history of the discussion.

## Decision

What did we choose? Be specific. Include enough detail that the decision is
unambiguous to a reader who was not in the discussion.

## Consequences

What follows from this — both good and bad? What is now harder to change?
What downstream work does this create?

## Alternatives considered

What else did we look at? Why did we not choose those? One or two sentences
per alternative — enough for a future reader to understand the choice space
without re-running the discussion.
